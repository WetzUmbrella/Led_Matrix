# NR-03 tap to hit response, 25 Nov 2026

Target (revised 24 Nov): tap to hit feedback under 300 ms. One-tile session on tile 5 (`pc/web.py --layout 1x1 --map 5 --policy continue`), game mode, easy level. The other 8 tiles were online and their echo TAPs reached the Pico as in a full wall.

## Method

Front-on phone video (iPhone, slow motion) cannot show the moment of contact: the finger hovers over the tile for about 0.5 s before each tap. So the delay is split into parts, each measured or bounded:

1. **Contact to accelerometer peak** (`t_tap`): at most 10 ms (peak window of the tap detector, step 2).
2. **`t_tap` to Pico arrival**: the Pico's own `GAME_TAP` event (`value` = arrival minus `t_tap`, synced clock), round 3, log `pico_nr03_round.log`.
3. **Collect wait**: 150 ms from the first arrival (`GAME_COLLECT_MS`), then FEEDBACK.
4. **FEEDBACK to visible squash**: video `IMG_3440.mov` (round 2), 240 fps capture (burst spacing 228 frames vs tap spacing 0.95 s in the tap log), 4.17 ms per frame. After a hit the Pico plans the next mole at `now + GAME_LEAD_MS` (200 ms), and tiles draw a mole at its `t_show` on the synced clock, so the next mole's first row marks FEEDBACK + 200 ms. Squash onset to next-mole onset was 47 frames (196 ms) on all 4 clean hits (bursts at frames 833, 1061, 1294, 1522), so the squash shows **about 4 ms (one frame)** after FEEDBACK. Later hits were hidden by the hand.

## Result

Tile 5, 39 taps (38 hits + start), `t_tap` to Pico arrival: min 14.9 ms, median 17.4 ms, p90 23.1 ms, max 32.5 ms.

| | Tap to squash |
|---|---|
| Median | 17 + 150 + 4 = **about 171 ms** (+ up to 10 ms contact to peak) |
| Worst tap | 32 + 150 + 4 + 10 = **about 196 ms** |

**NR-03 met: every tap under 200 ms, target 300 ms.** Round 3: 38 of 38 moles, 1 wrong, mean reaction 384 ms.

Limits: the display part rests on 4 hits; a one-tile session (fewer MOLE_ON and FEEDBACK messages than a 3x3 round, the same echo TAP traffic). Cross-check with 24 Nov (`pico_round_fix.log`, 9-tile wall): the first arrival of each tap (the one the collect wait starts from) had median 30 ms, p90 57 ms, max 103 ms, so the worst 24 Nov tap is about 103 + 150 + 4 + 10 = 267 ms, still under 300 ms. (All arrivals including the late echoes reached p90 124 ms, but echoes do not delay the FEEDBACK.)
