Grid size

Up to 9 x 9 cells; place up to 9 tiles in any of them.

Tiles on the wall

Empty cell: click, then tap the tile for that cell. Numbered cell: click to switch it off. Dashed cell (switched off): click to switch it back on.

Mode

Game

A round lasts 30 seconds. A mole pops up on one tile: a tap on it scores +1, any other tap scores -1. The score is shown here as it changes, and drawn across the wall at the end.
